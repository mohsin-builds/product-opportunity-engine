# Product Opportunity Engine

**Know if a product is worth selling — before you list it.**

Product Opportunity Engine is an interactive decision-support tool built by Mohsin Labs to help sellers evaluate product economics, pricing room, risk and market opportunity before committing inventory or capital.

## Live Product

**https://mohsinbuilds.com/product-opportunity-engine/**

## What It Does

The Product Opportunity Engine turns a set of product assumptions into a practical opportunity analysis.

Users can enter values such as:

- Selling price
- Supplier cost
- Shipping cost
- Marketplace fee
- Payment fee
- Risk allowance
- Other costs

The tool then calculates and visualizes whether the product economics look strong enough to investigate further.

## Core Features

### Opportunity Score

Generates an overall product opportunity score based on several economic and risk factors.

### Profit Economics

Calculates:

- Total cost
- Net profit
- Profit margin
- ROI

### Price Safety Zone

Shows key pricing levels including:

- Break-even price
- Safer pricing range
- Target pricing
- Current selling price

### Product Stress Test

Tests how the product performs when conditions become less favorable, such as:

- Higher supplier cost
- Higher shipping cost
- Increased marketplace fees
- Return scenarios

### Decision Intelligence

Summarizes the strongest parts of the product economics and highlights areas that deserve additional investigation.

### What-If Simulator

Allows users to adjust major assumptions and immediately see how the economics respond.

### Break-Even & Targets

Provides practical pricing thresholds to help users understand how much pricing room exists before profitability becomes weak.

### Marketplace Fit

Provides an economic-readiness view based on the selected marketplace and the assumptions entered by the user.

## Important Note

Product Opportunity Engine is a **decision-support tool**, not a source of live marketplace data.

Marketplace fees and cost assumptions are entered manually by the user. The tool does not currently retrieve live marketplace, supplier, competition or demand data.

Results are intended to support research and product evaluation rather than replace professional financial, marketplace or business analysis.
